# BYD equity valuation

Which assumptions have the greatest effect on an estimate of BYD’s equity value?

This 16-page team equity research report connects vertical integration and EV capacity investment to operating performance and free cash flow. It uses discounted cash flow as the primary valuation and price-to-book multiples as a cross-check.

Sensitivity analysis makes the dependence on discount rates and growth assumptions visible. This case study describes the historical valuation exercise. The supplied material is a finished report.

### Reading the valuation

The operating argument starts with vertical integration. Internal production can change unit costs, but building capacity also ties up capital. That distinction matters because sales growth and free cash flow can move in different directions. The report projects operating performance, discounts the resulting cash flows and checks the implied equity value against price-to-book multiples.

The sensitivity tables vary the discount rate and terminal growth rate. They make explicit which assumptions would have to hold for the historical valuation to be plausible.
