# Commodity futures trading journal

What does a trading record show that an end-of-period profit figure misses?

The simulation report traces trades in contracts including urea and red dates. It relates seasonality, production and demand to price trends, then compares turnover, average execution prices, open positions and floating profit or loss.

The journal uses four views of each trading record to distinguish a directional gain from the risk accumulated while holding a large position. It also considers how frequent trading and fees affected the simulated account.

### Two contracts from the journal

| Contract | Market question | What the simulation tracked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urea | How do fertilizer demand, production costs and export conditions interact? | Executions around trend changes, growth in open positions and the retracement in cumulative profit. |
| Red dates | How does the arrival of a new crop affect a market with seasonal consumption? | Position decisions against supply expectations and changes in demand after the holiday season. |
